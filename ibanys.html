Ben Nichols on why he built this program for community banks.

The problem

The distance between AI hype and AI execution

Most AI education for bank executives ends the same way. A certificate, a full notebook, and nothing your board can act on. You go back to the bank with better vocabulary and the same open question: so what do we actually do.

This program is built the other direction. Every session pairs a framework with hands on work against your own institution, and what you build across the six weeks becomes a single document you own.

It is specific to your bank because you wrote it. Which is also why you can defend it when a director asks the question you did not prepare for.

Not an academic survey. Not a vendor demo. Led by someone who deploys AI systems inside financial institutions.

What you leave with

An AI strategy and implementation roadmap

Built session by session against your own institution, not a template. The capstone is designed to secure internal buy in from your board, your executive team, and your budget committee.

Your instructor

Practitioner led, not lecture led

Ben Nichols is Founder and Principal Consultant at Upstate AI and Adjunct Professor of Data Science at Syracuse University. He builds and runs AI systems commercially inside financial institutions, which is the other four days of his week.

That is the difference this program is built on. The frameworks come from work that shipped, the failure modes come from ones that did not, and the answer to a question about your specific core provider is not a shrug.
