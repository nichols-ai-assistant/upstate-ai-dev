## 2. How They Make Money

SREG earns revenue across four distinct channels. These stack together for properties where they are both the operator and a JV co-owner.

### Revenue Stream 1: Property Management Fees (Largest, Recurring)

For third-party assignments, SREG charges a **management fee as a percentage of gross receipts**, typically 3–5% for commercial properties of this type. On a 900K SF mall generating (conservatively) $6–$10M in annual gross revenue, this yields $180K–$500K per property per year in management fees alone.

Across 40 properties, even at 3% on $7M average gross revenue each = **~$8.4M/year in base management fees**. With their premium institutional positioning they likely command 4–5%, pushing this toward $10–14M.

Additional management revenue:
- **Administrative/coordination fees** on top of base management (1–3%)
- **Construction/Tenant Coordination fees**: typically 5–10% of project cost; SREG explicitly lists "Construction & Tenant Coordination" as a service
- **CAM reimbursements**: in NNN mall leases, management fees are often a reimbursable expense billed back to tenants, making the management fee effectively pass-through for the landlord

### Revenue Stream 2: Leasing Commissions (Transactional, High-Margin)

Leasing commissions on enclosed mall leases follow a standard industry structure:
- **New leases**: ~6% of aggregate base rent for the first 5 years + 3% thereafter, split half on lease execution and half on tenant opening
- **Renewals**: 1–3% of renewal term value or a flat fee
- **Expansions**: 6% of incremental rent for balance of current term

Their 180% Q1 2024 leasing performance suggests this is a major revenue driver. Across a 40-property portfolio with active leasing activity — even modest new lease volume at 6% commission generates significant annual income.

**Example**: A 5-year lease at $50/SF on 5,000 SF = $1.25M aggregate rent → 6% = **$75,000 commission on one deal**. SREG described their LOI pipeline as "unprecedented" in company history as of Q1 2024.

## 4. AI Opportunities

SREG is already more data-forward than most mall operators. However, their AI maturity is still early-stage — they use data tools, not yet AI tools. Here are the highest-value AI opportunities, ranked by ROI proximity:

---

### Opportunity 1: AI-Powered Leasing Intelligence (Immediate, High ROI)

**The problem**: SREG's Leasing+™ differentiator is their leasing engine. But tenant targeting, deal pricing, and mix optimization still rely heavily on experienced judgment.

**The AI opportunity**: Layer a predictive leasing model on top of Yardi + Placer.ai data that answers:
- *Which specific tenants should we recruit for this specific vacancy?* (based on visitor demographics, cross-shopping patterns, existing mix gaps)
- *What rent can this space support?* (predictive NOI modeling by tenant category)
- *How long will this deal take to close?* (pipeline velocity forecasting)
- *What TI investment unlocks the best risk-adjusted return?* (TI spend vs. rent premium models)

**Industry evidence**: BCG research (2025) shows AI-enabled tenant management drove **50% increase in tenant revenue** for early-movers. A single property with 100+ tenants and $10M in annual rent has massive optimization upside.

**Fit for SREG**: Directly accelerates their Leasing+™ brand. They're already tracking all deal data in Yardi + Salesforce — this is a model training data goldmine.

### Opportunity 2: Portfolio-Level BI with AI Synthesis (Near-term)

**The problem**: SREG's BI team produces custom reports per property. Cross-portfolio pattern recognition — "what worked at Crossroads Center applies to Valley West Mall" — is a human task today.

**The AI opportunity**: An AI layer on top of their Yardi + Anaplan data that:
- Identifies which KPI combinations predict tenant performance deterioration 6 months ahead (early warning system)
- Surfaces which management interventions (new signage, remerchandising, marketing activation) drove occupancy gains at comparable properties
- Auto-generates property performance narrative reports for investor clients ("White Marsh Q1 summary: foot traffic up 12% vs. prior quarter, consistent with anchor Macy's expansion and new F&B tenants signed in February")
- Benchmarks each property against portfolio peers and industry benchmarks

**Industry evidence**: ICSC reports AI-driven analytics producing **11% traffic improvement** for shopping centers implementing predictive optimization. BCG: **20% staff efficiency improvement** from AI-synthesized data.

**Fit for SREG**: This becomes a client-facing differentiator in winning third-party assignments. "Here's what our AI-powered BI produces — our competitors don't have this."

### Opportunity 3: AI Lease Abstraction & Administration (Quick Win)

**The problem**: SREG administers leases for 40+ malls, each with 50–200+ tenants. Every lease is a dense legal document with critical dates (options, expirations, CAM caps, co-tenancy clauses, exclusivity provisions). Manual abstraction is slow, expensive, and error-prone.

**The AI opportunity**: AI lease abstraction tools (several exist: Kira Systems, Evisort, Docugami, or Yardi's own emerging AI features) that:
- Extract and normalize all key terms from PDFs automatically
- Flag critical upcoming dates (expirations, rent bumps, option notices)
- Identify co-tenancy trigger risks (if anchor X closes, does inline tenant Y get rent relief or termination rights?)
- Ensure percentage rent clauses are being tracked and billed correctly

**Industry evidence**: JLL's Lease Navigator (multi-agent AI system) handles this at scale for their occupier clients. Colliers' AI platform reduced manual lease admin hours dramatically. Accruent cites AI lease abstraction as reducing data overload and catching missed obligations.

**Fit for SREG**: With a 40+ property portfolio where CAM reconciliations alone generate hundreds of hours annually, this is pure efficiency. Frees up the property accounting team for analysis instead of data entry. Also reduces liability (missed co-tenancy triggers can be costly).

### Opportunity 4: Predictive Maintenance & Energy Optimization (Medium-term)

**The problem**: Enclosed malls are massive physical assets — averaging 900K+ SF at SREG. HVAC, elevators, escalators, roofing, and parking structures require constant maintenance. Reactive maintenance is the industry default.

**The AI opportunity**: IoT sensor networks + AI predictive maintenance that:
- Predicts HVAC failures before they occur (based on vibration, temperature, energy draw patterns)
- Optimizes HVAC scheduling for crowd patterns (Placer.ai foot traffic data + building automation = smart HVAC)
- Identifies energy waste in real-time across all systems
- Prioritizes work orders by predicted urgency and cost of failure

**Industry evidence**: NAIOP cites JLL's Hank AI platform achieving **20%+ energy cost reduction** at commercial buildings. At $1–3/SF in energy costs for a 1M SF enclosed mall, a 20% reduction = **$200K–$600K annual savings per property**.

**Fit for SREG**: Across 40 properties, the fleet-wide energy savings alone could exceed $5–10M annually. This also directly improves NOI — which is how SREG is compensated on performance-based management structures and how their JV equity grows.

**Fit for SREG**: They are disciplined opportunistic acquirers. An AI deal-sourcing and underwriting pipeline would let their small team process far more opportunities and close faster — a genuine edge in a market where speed matters.

### Opportunity 6: Generative AI for Leasing & Marketing Content (Quick Win)

**The problem**: Every property needs a leasing package, LOI templates, deal summaries, broker co-op marketing, and digital/social content. This is high-volume, repetitive creative and document work.

**The AI opportunity**:

---

### Opportunity 7: Tenant Percentage Rent Audit Automation

**The problem**: Most enclosed mall leases include **percentage rent** clauses — tenants pay base rent plus a percentage of gross sales above a "natural breakpoint." SREG must collect tenant sales reports, audit them for accuracy, and bill percentage rent correctly. This is error-prone and often under-billed.

**The AI opportunity**:
- Automatically ingest tenant sales reports (email attachments, tenant portals, Yardi CommercialCafe)
- Compare reported sales to Placer.ai foot traffic trends as a sanity check (if traffic is up 20% but tenant reports flat sales — flag for audit)
- Calculate percentage rent due, flag underreporting
- Identify which tenants are approaching their natural breakpoint (and thus about to generate incremental income)

**Fit for SREG**: Virtually zero incremental revenue capture today that AI makes routine. On a large mall with 150 tenants, even a 2–3% improvement in percentage rent billing accuracy could add $200K–$500K per property annually to the landlord's NOI.

SREG is making a contrarian bet that the mainstream media narrative about "dying malls" is overblown. Their thesis, backed by BCG, ICSC, and Cushman & Wakefield research:
- Physical retail vacancy is at **4.3%** nationally (Q2 2025) — historically low
- New retail construction fell to **30M SF in 2024** from 52M SF in 2023 — supply is constrained
- Physical stores remain the most profitable channel for retailers
- Enclosed malls that serve as **regional anchors with no nearby competition** are particularly durable

SREG specifically targets malls that are: (a) the only enclosed center within 50+ miles, (b) serving a large, underserved trade area, and (c) financially distressed due to mismanagement or debt structure — not fundamental demand problems.
