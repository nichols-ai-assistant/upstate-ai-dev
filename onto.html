The Problem

Confident answers, whether right or wrong.

Teams already ask AI assistants regulatory compliance questions. The model answers confidently either way. In financial services, that is not an inconvenience; it is an exam finding.

Hallucinated citations

The model invents references that look plausible and do not exist. Your compliance function inherits the cleanup, or your examiner finds it first.

Outdated rules, wrong references

Model training data lags the regulation. An answer built on a superseded rule is worse than no answer, because it arrives with confidence.

The build-it-yourself trap

The alternative, building and maintaining your own retrieval stack, means a data pipeline, an eval program, and an engineering team you did not budget for.
