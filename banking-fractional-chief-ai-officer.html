An advisor who knows the context.

You should not have to explain your core, your current projects, and your constraints from the beginning every time an AI question comes up. The engagement builds that working knowledge so advice connects to the decisions your institution is already making.

What the engagement includes.

  • First-month discovery that produces a one-page AI Opportunity Map.

  • A monthly strategy call to set priorities and work through the current question.

  • Review of AI vendor proposals, contracts, and roadmaps before you commit.

  • Board and leadership briefing support, from reviewing materials to preparing talking points.

Work through the next decision.

Depending on the priorities agreed with your team, conversations may include:

  • What to ask your core or digital banking provider about an AI feature and the information it uses.

  • How a proposed staff use fits your acceptable-use expectations and existing review process.

  • What evidence leadership needs before continuing, changing, or stopping a pilot.

  • How to explain a recommendation, its dependencies, and its trade-offs to the board.

Keep ownership inside the institution.

We work alongside your executives, IT, operations, and risk teams. Your institution retains approval authority and responsibility for its policies and decisions. Our role is to bring independent AI judgment, document the reasoning, and help your team build its own capability.
